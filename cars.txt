Cars are one of the most common modes of transportation in the world.
The first gasoline-powered car was invented by Karl Benz in 1885.
Modern cars come in many types such as sedans, SUVs, and electric vehicles.
Electric cars are becoming more popular due to environmental concerns.
The fastest production car in the world can exceed 300 miles per hour.
Cars have safety features like airbags, seatbelts, and anti-lock brakes.
The automotive industry employs millions of people worldwide.
Self-driving cars are being developed by many technology companies.
